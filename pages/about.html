About us

Kita Tolong Kita (KTK) is a student services platform. We connect students who need everyday help, starting with laundry and ironing, with students who want to earn on their own schedule.

Mission

Make daily life easier for students and create small, fair income opportunities inside the campus community.

Values

  • Community first
  • Fair and transparent pricing
  • Trust and accountability

Target audience

University students living on or near campus, and students who want to become service providers.
